Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

What LUTHUR is

LUTHUR is a personal assistant app that runs entirely on the owner's own computer. There is no LUTHUR server, account system or cloud database.

Google data it accesses

Only when the owner connects a Google account and approves Google's consent screen, LUTHUR may access:

  • Gmail: to show emails in the dashboard and, if the owner turns on writing, to send emails the owner has reviewed and approved.
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets: to list, search and preview files and, if the owner turns on writing, to create files and make edits the owner confirms.
  • Basic profile (email address and name): to label the connected account.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is fetched directly from Google to the owner's computer and shown only to the owner.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Email and file contents are not stored, except for short-lived caches in the app's local data folder.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Nothing is sent, edited or deleted without the owner's explicit action. LUTHUR cannot delete emails or files.

LUTHUR's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect an account at any time inside LUTHUR (Workspace → Accounts → Remove), or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing it deletes the stored sign-in from the computer.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on LUTHUR's Google sign-in screen.
